Fewer false wakes, and Yossi no longer takes over the microphone.

- **False-wake check.** After "Hey Yossi" is heard, a second, separately trained model has to agree before Yossi
  wakes. Ordinary speech (dictation, reading aloud) no longer sets it off. Settings > Advanced > False-wake check:
  On / Log only / Off.
- **No surprise listening.** After a reply, the mic re-opens only when Yossi has asked you something.
- **Leaves the mic to other apps.** While any other app is recording (a voice recorder, a USB microphone, a call),
  Yossi stops listening for the wake word and starts again when the other app is done. Yossi itself always listens on
  the phone's own mic, and only takes audio focus while it is speaking.
- **Wake clips.** Every wake is saved (kept or rejected) to help retrain "Hey Yossi". Settings > Advanced > Share
  wake clips. Set "Speaker on clips" to your name.
- The tuning settings moved to Settings > Advanced.
