Use Yossi without the wake word.

- **Widget.** A home screen widget: one tap opens the chat and starts listening. Add it from Settings > Add the Yossi
  widget to the home screen. It works with the wake word off and with listening paused.
- **Wake word switch on the main screen.** Off stops the wake word and nothing else.
- **Chat thread.** The conversation is shown as a chat instead of only the last reply.
- The listening on/off is now at the bottom of Settings (and still in Quick Settings).
- Rotating the phone, or reopening Yossi from Recents, no longer starts listening again.
